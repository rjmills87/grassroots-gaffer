# Welcome to Grassroots Gaffer! ⚽

Thank you for being one of our first test users! This guide will help you get started with the app.

## What is Grassroots Gaffer?

Grassroots Gaffer is an ad-free, intuitive web app designed specifically for grassroots football coaches and parents. It helps you:

- **Manage your team roster** - Add players and their guardian contact details
- **Schedule events** - Create matches and training sessions with dates, times, and locations
- **Track availability** - See at a glance who can attend each event
- **Communicate** - Send team announcements and reminders

**No ads. No clutter. Just what you need.**

## Getting Started

### For Coaches

#### 1. Create Your Account

1. Visit the registration page
2. Enter your name, email, and create a password
3. Click "Coach Registration"
4. Verify your email address (check your inbox)

#### 2. Create Your First Team

1. Once logged in, you'll see your dashboard
2. Click "Create Team" button
3. Fill in:
   - **Team Name** (e.g., "U12 Eagles")
   - **Age Group** (select from dropdown)
   - **Team Badge** (optional - upload a team logo)
4. Click "Create Team"

#### 3. Add Players to Your Roster

1. Click on your team to open the team page
2. Scroll to "Squad List" section
3. Click "Add Player" button
4. Fill in player details:
   - Player name
   - Guardian name
   - Guardian email (this is important - they'll receive welcome emails)
   - Guardian phone
   - Squad number (optional)
   - Position (optional)
5. Click "Add Player"

**What happens next?** The guardian will receive a welcome email with instructions to set up their account.

#### 4. Schedule an Event

1. On your team page, scroll to "Events" section
2. Click "Create Event" button
3. Fill in event details:
   - **Event Type** (Match or Practice)
   - **Date and Time**
   - **Location**
   - **Details** (optional - any additional information)
4. Click "Create Event"

**Note:** All players on your roster are automatically added to new events, so guardians can respond immediately.

#### 5. Send Team Messages

1. On your team page, scroll to "Messages" section
2. Click "Create Message" button
3. Type your message (max 255 characters)
4. Click "Send Message"

Your message will be visible to all team members (coaches and guardians).

#### 6. View Player Availability

1. Click on any upcoming event (from dashboard or team page)
2. You'll see a list of all players
3. Each player's status will show:
   - **Attending** (green) - Player confirmed they can attend
   - **Unavailable** (red) - Player confirmed they cannot attend
   - **No Response** - Guardian hasn't responded yet

#### 7. Send Reminders

If some guardians haven't responded to an event:

1. Open the event detail page
2. Click "Send Reminder" button
3. Only guardians who haven't responded will receive a reminder email

#### 8. Edit or Delete Messages

- **Edit:** Click "Edit Message" on any message you created, make changes, then "Save Message"
- **Delete:** Click "Delete Message" on any message you created (you'll be asked to confirm)

### For Guardians/Parents

#### 1. Set Up Your Account

When a coach adds your child to their team, you'll receive a welcome email:

1. Check your email inbox for the welcome message
2. Click the password reset link in the email
3. Create a secure password
4. You're ready to log in!

#### 2. Log In

1. Visit the login page
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click "Log In"

#### 3. View Your Dashboard

Your dashboard shows:
- **Your Teams** - All teams where your child(ren) are players
- **Upcoming Events** - Next matches and training sessions
- **Recent Messages** - Latest team announcements

#### 4. Respond to Event Availability

1. Click on an upcoming event (from dashboard or team page)
2. Find your child's name in the player list
3. Click either:
   - **"Attending"** - Your child can attend
   - **"Unavailable"** - Your child cannot attend

Your response is saved immediately and the coach can see it right away.

#### 5. View Team Information

- Click on any team to see:
  - Team details and badge
  - Full squad list
  - All upcoming events
  - Team messages from the coach

## Tips & Best Practices

### For Coaches

- **Add all players first** - This ensures everyone gets added to future events automatically
- **Schedule events early** - Give guardians plenty of time to respond
- **Use team messages** - Great for general announcements, last-minute changes, or team updates
- **Send reminders** - Don't hesitate to send reminders for important matches
- **Keep details updated** - Update player information if contact details change

### For Guardians

- **Respond promptly** - Coaches need to know availability for planning
- **Check regularly** - New events and messages appear on your dashboard
- **Update your password** - Keep your account secure with a strong password
- **Contact the coach** - If you have questions, reach out directly (the app shows contact details)

## Common Questions

### How do I change my password?

1. Log in to your account
2. Click on your profile/name in the top right
3. Go to "Settings" → "Password"
4. Enter your current password and new password
5. Save changes

### Can I be a guardian for multiple players?

Yes! If you have multiple children on different teams (or the same team), you'll see all their teams on your dashboard.

### What if I don't receive the welcome email?

- Check your spam/junk folder
- Verify the email address the coach used is correct
- Contact the coach to resend or verify your email address
- You can also use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page

### Can coaches see my phone number?

Yes, coaches can see guardian contact details (name, email, phone) for all players on their team. This helps them contact you if needed.

### How do I delete my account?

Currently, account deletion must be done by contacting support. This feature may be added in the future.

### Is my data secure?

Yes! We take data security seriously:
- Passwords are encrypted
- SSL encryption for all data transmission
- No third-party advertising or data selling
- Your data is only visible to you and your team's coach
